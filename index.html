How Much Do Managed IT Services Cost in Houston? (2026 Guide with Real Price Ranges)

Real Houston per-user pricing by tier, company size and pricing model — what is included, what is billed extra, and how to spot a quote that is too cheap to be true.

The short answer

In Houston, managed IT services cost $100 to $250 per user per month in 2026 for a fully managed plan. Most small and mid-sized Houston businesses land in the standard tier at $125 to $175 per user per month, which covers help desk support, 24/7 monitoring, patching, endpoint security and backup. A 25-person company therefore typically pays $2,500 to $6,250 per month, before Microsoft 365 licensing and project work, which are usually billed separately.

Key takeaways

  • Standard fully managed IT in Houston: $125–$175 per user/month; most businesses land near $138–$150.
  • Full market range: $100–$250 per user/month, depending on security and compliance depth.
  • Co-managed IT (supporting an in-house IT person): $60–$130 per user/month.
  • Compliance-heavy industries (healthcare, finance, energy): $175–$300 per user/month.
  • Break-fix / hourly IT support: $150–$200 per hour, with no monitoring or security included.
  • Onboarding fees in Houston typically equal 1 month of the recurring fee (up to 2 months for complex environments).
  • Microsoft 365 licensing is extra — and it rose on 1 July 2026 to $7/user/month (Business Basic) and $14/user/month (Business Standard).

What is included in a standard managed IT plan?

At the $125–$175 standard tier in Houston, a mature MSP bundles five categories into the monthly fee:

  1. Help desk & user support — remote and on-site support for day-to-day issues.
  2. Monitoring & maintenance — 24/7 network and endpoint monitoring, patching and updates, so problems are fixed before they cause downtime.
  3. Cybersecurity stack — endpoint detection and response (EDR), multi-factor authentication (MFA), firewall management, email security and threat monitoring.
  4. Backup & disaster recovery — scheduled backups, recovery planning and testing. In Houston this is not optional: hurricane season and flooding make tested recovery a business-survival issue, not an IT luxury.
  5. IT strategy — a vCIO or dedicated support manager, reporting, asset tracking and a technology roadmap aligned to your budget.

What is usually NOT included (budget for these separately)

  • Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace licences — billed per user, and Microsoft raised prices on 1 July 2026.
  • VoIP phone systems and minutes.
  • Hardware — laptops, servers, firewalls and the labour to replace them.
  • Projects — office moves, cloud migrations, new line-of-business software rollouts.
  • Onboarding — typically a one-time fee equal to 1 month of service (up to 2 months for complex or compliance-heavy environments).
  • Compliance audits themselves — your MSP supports HIPAA/PCI/CMMC readiness, but formal audits are separate.

A quote that looks cheap is usually cheap because one of these lines was quietly removed.

7 factors that move your Houston quote up or down

  1. Number of users and locations — more sites across the Houston metro (Downtown, Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands) means more network gear to manage.
  2. Security & compliance requirements — HIPAA (Texas Medical Center and clinics), PCI for payments, and energy-sector controls add SOC monitoring, logging and documentation.
  3. Support hours — business-hours cover costs less than true 24/7 cover.
  4. Servers and legacy systems — on-premise servers and old line-of-business software cost more to maintain than a clean Microsoft 365 environment.
  5. On-site support needs — field crews, warehouses and multi-site operations need more on-site visits.
  6. Backup & recovery targets — how fast you must be back up after an outage (hours vs. minutes) changes the backup stack price.
  7. Contract term — longer terms usually lock a lower rate; month-to-month flexibility costs a little more.

Managed IT vs. hiring in-house IT in Houston

A single in-house IT generalist typically costs a Houston business roughly $88,000–$150,000 per year fully loaded (salary, payroll taxes, benefits, tools and training) — and still only covers business hours, takes holidays, and cannot be an expert in security, cloud, networking and compliance at once. A managed IT agreement for a 25-person business costs $30,000–$75,000 per year and includes a whole team, 24/7 monitoring and a security stack. That is why most Houston businesses under ~100 employees outsource IT entirely, and larger ones use co-managed IT to extend their internal hire.

Red flags: when a Houston IT quote is too cheap

  • Under $100 per user/month for "full" management — something essential (EDR, backup testing, after-hours cover) is almost always missing.
  • No onboarding fee and no discovery process — the provider has not looked at your environment, so surprises will be billed later.
  • No written list of exclusions — licensing, projects and after-hours work will appear as extras.
  • No backup testing — an untested backup is a hope, not a recovery plan, and Houston weather tests it every year.

What does per-user pricing mean for managed IT?

Per-user pricing is a fixed monthly fee for each employee the MSP supports, regardless of how many devices that person uses. In Houston it ranges from $100 to $250 per user per month in 2026, and it is the most common model because it makes IT cost scale predictably with headcount.

What is included in managed IT services at the standard price?

A standard Houston managed IT plan ($125–$175 per user/month) typically includes help desk support, 24/7 monitoring, patch management, endpoint security (EDR), multi-factor authentication, data backup and disaster recovery, and IT strategy or vCIO guidance. Co-managed IT in Houston costs $60 to $130 per user per month, with most companies paying $85 to $110. The MSP provides monitoring tools, security stack, patching and escalation support while your internal IT staff handle first-line, day-to-day requests.

Is there an onboarding fee for managed IT services?

Yes. Houston MSPs typically charge a one-time onboarding fee equal to about one month of the recurring service fee — for example, $4,000 to $8,000 on a $4,000/month agreement for a complex environment. It covers discovery, documentation, tool deployment and security hardening.
